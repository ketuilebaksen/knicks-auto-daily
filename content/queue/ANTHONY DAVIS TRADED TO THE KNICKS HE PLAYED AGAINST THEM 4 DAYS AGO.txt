Now let me answer it, in four parts.

The Knicks are well above the first apron and only about three point two million under the second apron, which ownership treats as a hard ceiling. So a straight Towns-for-Davis swap doesn't work. New York has to add a small contract to make the numbers legal. And the cheapest contracts on the roster mostly belong to players who signed this summer, who can't be traded until December 15th.
