Let me explain why the 2033 pick is the only first the Knicks can trade, because it's one of the most misunderstood details in all of these videos. The Knicks owe their 2027, 2029 and 2031 first-round picks to Brooklyn. The NBA has a rule, called the Stepien rule, that says a team can't be without its own first-round pick in two consecutive future drafts.
