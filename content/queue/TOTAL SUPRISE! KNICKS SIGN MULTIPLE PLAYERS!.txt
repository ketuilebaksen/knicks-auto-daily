Now, before we go further, I want to explain something that almost nobody is clarifying — and it completely changes how you read this whole list.

These are Exhibit 9 contracts. Not Exhibit 10s.

And those are two different things.

An Exhibit 10, which is what Igor Milicic signed, is a pathway deal. It can convert to a two-way contract, and if the player gets waived and joins the G League affiliate, he collects a bonus. It's for developmental guys the organization wants in its pipeline.

An Exhibit 9 is different. It's a camp contract with injury protection — essentially a one-day minimum if a player gets hurt in camp. It doesn't convert to a two-way. It exists so a team can fill out a camp roster with veterans without taking on real risk.

So look at who got which deal.

Milicic, twenty-four years old with development upside, got the Exhibit 10. Wiseman, Brown, Agbaji, Konchar, and Eubanks all got Exhibit 9s.

That tells you the front office is running two separate competitions at once. One for the two-way spots, aimed at young players. And one for that single fourteenth roster spot, aimed at veterans. They're not competing against each other. They're competing in separate lanes.
