And I want to explain that last one, because a lot of people hear "waive and stretch" and tune out. It's actually the key to this whole story.

Stretching a contract means a team releases a player and spreads his remaining salary across multiple future seasons instead of eating it all at once. It lowers your cap hit today at the cost of carrying dead money later. And here's the cold-blooded part — the player you stretch is almost always the one who can't help you right now. Because why would you pay a full cap hit for somebody who isn't going to be on the floor?
