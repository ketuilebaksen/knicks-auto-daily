And here's why this pairing actually works. Towns and Drummond don't duplicate each other at all.

With Towns on the floor, your center spaces beyond the arc, scores inside, rebounds, and passes out of the frontcourt. With Drummond, your center screens, rolls hard, finishes at the rim, attacks the offensive glass, and occupies bodies in the paint.
