Now before we go further, let me actually explain what an Exhibit 10 is — because the term gets thrown around constantly and almost nobody explains it, and it's the entire reason this signing makes sense.

An Exhibit 10 is a one-year, non-guaranteed minimum contract. It costs the team essentially nothing. You bring the player to camp, you watch him, and then one of three things happens.

You convert him to a two-way contract. You keep him on the standard roster. Or you waive him — and if he then joins your G League affiliate and stays sixty days, he collects a bonus.

So it's a free look for the team, and a real pathway for the player. Which explains exactly why he chose us.
